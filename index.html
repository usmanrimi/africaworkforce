The problem

Africa's education system and its employers are not speaking. Nigeria's TVET enrolment sits below 10% of tertiary education, while employers report graduates who lack hands-on skills, safety culture, and work-readiness. The bridge between academic training and real employment is broken.

The Status Quo

  • Years-long, school-like and theoretical programmes
  • Isolated from industry — no employer co-design
  • Assessment by written exam only
  • Static curricula that don't reflect market demand
  • TVETs perceived as second-tier and under-resourced
  • Long time-to-productivity after hiring

The AWTD Blueprint

  • Months-long, short-cycle & stackable modules
  • Employers co-design competencies from day one
  • Performance-based portfolios & certifications
  • Built for rapid iteration and replication
  • Workshop-first: hands-on before theory
  • Paid apprenticeships with employer mentors

The model

Industry-led, competency-based, and measured on outcomes. A public-private model that co-designs training with employers and verifies skills through practical assessments. Every element is engineered to prevent the programme from becoming theoretical or school-like.

The learner journey

Every learner moves through the same six stages, in the same order. Capstone Validation is a gate; nobody is credentialled without clearing it.

Intake

Application + Audition Week screening: attendance, punctuality, aptitude — not standard tests.

2-Step Process

2–4 Weeks

High-demand, trade-specific hands-on instruction. 70% practical, 30% guided theory.

8–16 Weeks

8–24 Weeks

Non-Negotiable Gate

Transition Support

Job matching, interview practice, or microenterprise kits for self-employment pathways.

Operating principles

These six govern what goes into the curriculum and what stays out. They aren't negotiable with partners, funders or employers.

Employment Outcomes First

Training is designed backward from real jobs, real tools, and measurable placement and wage gains. If it doesn't lead to work, it doesn't belong in the curriculum.

Industry-Aligned Excellence

Employers co-design competencies, assessments, and credentials to ensure relevance and quality. Quarterly refresh cycles keep standards current with market demand.

Access and Equity

Affordable, inclusive pathways that expand opportunity for youth, women, and underserved communities. Targeted supports to reach those excluded from traditional education routes.

Hands-On Mastery

Competency-based learning grounded in practice, safety, and demonstrated performance — not seat time. 70% of every programme is workshop-based.

Stackable Pathways

Clear, modular credentials that enable learners to earn quickly and return to advance over time. Career mobility built into the architecture from the start.

Accountability & Improvement

Data-driven decisions, transparent standards, and rapid iteration based on results. We publish what we measure — including what isn't working.

Partners

AWTD does not operate in isolation. The model works because each partner has a defined role, a real stake, and a measurable contribution. We start with an Employer Advisory Group of 3–5 committed employers to prove results before expanding.
